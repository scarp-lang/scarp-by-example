# Third-party example provenance

The initial Scarp programs and matching stdout fixtures were copied from the [official Scarp compiler repository](https://github.com/scarp-lang/scarp/tree/main/examples), inspected at commit `e4db8e191cd7ea623123cae7348450ca6c95b170`. They are derived from source distributed under **Apache License 2.0**, reproduced here in [LICENSE-APACHE-2.0](LICENSE-APACHE-2.0).

The learning repository's own original editorial material and tooling are MIT-licensed under [LICENSE](LICENSE), while upstream-derived example sources and output fixtures retain their upstream Apache-2.0 licensing and attribution. The origin of each initial lesson can be identified from the same stem under `scarp/examples/`.

The 2026-11-02 course expansion imported the following additional compiler fixtures from the same upstream source tree (unchanged between pinned compiler commit `e4db8e1` and inspected `9e50075`): `scopes`, `string_bindings`, `output`, `unicode`, `unicode_text`, `scalar_conversions`, `escapes`, `function_contracts`, `constant_expressions`, and `score_selection`. The `constant_expressions.srp` import prefix changed to `_support/` to match this repository's layout. Its upstream helpers `modules/constants.srp` and `modules/constant_base.srp` are retained under `05-modules/_support/`.

The next numerical-learning expansion imported eight additional upstream `.srp` programs **and byte-exact matching `.stdout` fixtures** from the same pinned compiler commit `e4db8e191cd7ea623123cae7348450ca6c95b170`: `float_smoothing`, `tile_windows`, `numerical_kernels`, `vector_probabilities`, `matrix_targets`, `matrix_forward`, `log_space` and `coefficient_fit`. They appear as numbered lessons in chapters 04 and 06 and remain Apache-2.0-licensed upstream material; the English and Hindi teaching commentary is original MIT-licensed editorial work.

The language-fundamentals expansion added four more upstream compiler fixtures from exactly the same pinned development revision: `comments`, `bit_fields`, `control_escapes` and `terminal_recursion`. Their original source and byte-exact output are published as numbered beginner and function lessons under the upstream **Apache-2.0 license**; bilingual editorial explanations remain MIT. The `control_escapes.stdout` fixture intentionally contains a raw carriage-return byte, which is not a CRLF line ending.

The next numerical-curriculum batch added six more unmodified upstream compiler programs with matching byte-exact output fixtures, sourced from the same pinned `e4db8e191cd7ea623123cae7348450ca6c95b170` revision: `scaled_norm`, `float_refinement`, `vector_allocation`, `linear_transform`, `matrix_classification`, and `matrix_probabilities`. They are assigned new lesson numbers in chapters 04 and 06, preserving upstream Apache-2.0 licensing; new bilingual explanation text is original MIT-licensed material. The upstream `vector_allocation` source includes an implementation-specific byte count as a comment; the lesson does not promise a general memory-layout API.

The first example requiring the later published Scarp `v0.0.5-alpha.3` source (`7715c28df09c7703b39e14fddb963275b25df476`) is the **text construction** lesson `examples/01-basics/16-text-construction.srp`, copied unchanged with exact `.stdout` from upstream `examples/text_construction.*`. This upstream program and output remain Apache-2.0-licensed. The new English/Hindi teaching text is original MIT-licensed editorial work. Earlier pinned `e4db8e1` builds may lack these new string-producing intrinsics.

Do not remove existing copyright notices. Review additional third-party material and its license before merging it.


The 2026-11-03 module-learning expansion copies three upstream entry examples at the **published Scarp v0.0.5-alpha.3 source commit `7715c28df09c7703b39e14fddb963275b25df476`**: `examples/collection_module.srp`, `examples/module_inspection.srp`, and `examples/inspection_json.srp`, together with their byte-exact `.stdout` files. All imported source and stdout remains **Apache-2.0-licensed upstream material**; the English/Hindi teaching material and original validation tests are MIT-licensed. The pinned compiler's three-platform CI is responsible for verifying the relocated imports and exact output bytes.
